<div class="bio-personal">
  <h2>Beyond the Resume</h2>
  <div class="bio-personal-text">
    <p>My academic and professional interests sit at the intersection of Composition Studies, Intelligence Studies, and Information Sciences, three intertwined and interdisciplinary fields that all approach how information circulates to where it is needed most. I'm fascinated by the idea of how humans learn, apply, and share knowledge, especially in the information overload of the digital age. My path started through undergraduate studies focusing on cultural and linguistic anthropology, and my coursework in UCF's Writing & Rhetoric and Texts & Technology programs helped broaden that scope to include discourses happening across communities.</p>

    <p>I'm a U.S. Army veteran who spent nearly seven years as an All Source Intelligence Analyst. My atypical career started at III Corps headquarters as part of a large fusion center supporting installation- and theatre-wide operations. My second duty station took me to Belgium and Supreme Headquarters Allied Powers Europe, where I worked as the Iran and Kosovo desks supporting NATO's Allied Command Counterintelligence's 19 field offices. I served one tour in Afghanistan as a Targeting NCO at ISAF Joint Command, and I'm incredibly proud of the opportunities I've had to work alongside professionals across our allied partners in NATO and other partnerships.</p>

    <p>When I'm not hunting down influence campaigns or untangling information problems, I gravitate toward longform media: YouTube essays in the background, adding to over 130 days of listening time on Audible, and the steady rhythms of older online games that suit my schedule far better than many modern titles. In and out of the office, I'm a warm, curious, and candid individual who feels a responsibility to always volunteer to be the solution. I care deeply about both the missions and the people I work with, and I maintain a quiet and discrete professionalism that makes "About Me" sections unexpectedly challenging compared to the other work I'm known for.</p>
